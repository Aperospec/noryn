Noryn

From research to knowledge, and from knowledge to better methods.

Why methodology

Methodology is the center of Noryn.

Research papers, literature, and data give us knowledge. From that foundation, people develop methodologies: ways to understand problems, apply knowledge, make judgments, and reach goals. Methods put knowledge to work and can be tested and improved through practice. I believe our most valuable ability is to create better methodologies from what we know and the data we hold. Methodology is what moves the world forward.

Skills sit at the execution end of this chain: the concrete operations a method needs in a particular tool and environment. They change fastest and are the easiest part for AI to take over. People should focus on sources, knowledge, and the ability to create better methods from them.

A knowledge base of your own

The research and data a person can access, understand, and keep building on become their own knowledge base. What will matter is whether we can draw on that knowledge and data to create better methodologies.

Noryn keeps each person’s project database in a local folder they choose. Privacy is part of this design: Noryn has no central server holding everyone’s projects and does not upload your library to a Noryn server.

What Noryn is for

Noryn is a Mac app in development, organized around methodology. It is being built to help people develop and revise methods from identifiable sources, let connected AI agents use those methods in real tasks, and use visible work and results to improve them further. AI can handle the changing operations; people retain their knowledge and methods.
