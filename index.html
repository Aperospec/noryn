Your knowledge. Your methods.

Why methodology

I believe human progress depends on our ability to develop better methodologies.

Research papers, literature, and data preserve what we have learned about the world. A methodology turns that knowledge into choices we can test: which questions to ask, which path to take, and how to judge the result. A skill is the ability to carry out part of that method with a particular tool or process. Change the tool, and the skill changes with it.

The industrial age made repeatable skills central to production and to the way people were trained. Work was broken into operations that could be taught, measured, and repeated. People learned to fit a process with the regularity expected of a machine. That model shaped more than a century of work, but it need not define human worth.

AI is increasingly able to take on those concrete operations. If we still organize human work by the standards of a machine, we will have given an old division of labor a new tool and missed the change AI could make possible. People should have room to leave work that consists only of executing skills behind: to work with knowledge, ask questions, develop methodologies, and revise them through practice.

A knowledge base of your own

A methodology needs a body of knowledge it can answer to. Papers, documents, and data become a personal knowledge base when someone can keep them, examine them, and build on them over time. With that base in hand, a person can challenge an existing process and develop an approach of their own.

That knowledge base should belong to the person who builds it. Noryn keeps each person's project database in a local folder they choose. It has no central server collecting everyone's projects, and it does not upload your library to a Noryn server. Privacy is part of what makes a knowledge base truly yours.

What Noryn is for

Noryn makes methodology the center of the product. It is designed to connect sources, methods, and actual work: a person develops and revises methods from their own knowledge base, AI carries out concrete operations under those methods, and the work itself supplies evidence for improving them. Tools and skills can change; the knowledge, methods, and judgment that guide them stay with the person.
